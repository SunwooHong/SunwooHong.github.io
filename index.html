I am an M.S. student at KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Se-Young Yun. In 2026, I was a visiting researcher at the Vector Institute and the University of Toronto, working with Prof. Rahul G. Krishnan. I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University.

I want to close the gap between what people do easily and what models still cannot.

Rotate a Sudoku board and a person sees the same puzzle; a language model that just solved it often fails. A person knows where a thrown ball will land without picturing every instant in between; most world models still generate every frame.

People reason instead of matching patterns, learn from very little, and act in a physical world they understand. I work on bringing models closer to each: reasoning, efficient learning, and world models for embodied agents.

Pivot-SD: Efficient Self-Distillation for Masked Diffusion Language Models

Seo Hyun Kim*, Sunwoo Hong*, Younwoo Choi, Chen-Hao Chao, Se-Young Yun, Rahul G. Krishnan

EMNLP 2026, Main Conference

A masked diffusion language model can be post-trained on under 4% of its tokens. Supervising only the few commitments that most reduce uncertainty beats full-sequence fine-tuning and online RL at a fraction of the compute.

KLASS: KL-Guided Fast Inference in Masked Diffusion Models

Seo Hyun Kim*, Sunwoo Hong*, Hojung Jung, Youngrok Park, Se-Young Yun

NeurIPS 2025

A training-free sampler that uses the KL divergence between denoising steps to find tokens that are safe to unmask in parallel. Up to 2.78× faster generation while improving over greedy decoding, across text, image, and molecular generation.

Logical Equivariance in Diffusion Language Models

Sunwoo Hong*, Seo Hyun Kim*, Chen-Hao Chao, Younwoo Choi, Se-Young Yun, Rahul G. Krishnan

Under review. Earlier version at the ICML 2026 Workshop on Foundations of Deep Generative Models

Accuracy on one presentation of a problem hides failures on logically equivalent ones. We measure whether correctness survives answer-preserving rewrites, and a post-training recipe raises the all-view correctness of LLaDA-8B from 47.7% to 86.1%.

Route-Guided CBF-QP Repair for Safe Vision-Language-Action Models

Chan Park, Sunwoo Hong, Kirak Kim, Yoonjeong Park, Alexander W. Olson, Ali Pesaranghader

RSS 2026 Workshop on Trustworthy Embodied Foundation Models

A repair module that adds route guidance to a CBF-QP safety filter, so a vision-language-action policy can rejoin its task trajectory after avoiding an obstacle.

Sunwoo Hong

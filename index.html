About

Hi, I'm Sunwoo.

I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

I am an M.S. student at KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Se-Young Yun. In 2026 I was a visiting researcher at the University of Toronto and the Vector Institute, hosted by Prof. Rahul G. Krishnan. I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University.

I look for the assumption that bottlenecks a class of generative models and replace it with a better-matched signal or objective. So far I have done this for masked diffusion language models. I am now bringing the same approach to world models and physical reasoning for embodied agents.

  • World models and physical reasoning
    • World models that predict at physical events instead of at fixed time steps
    • Visual and compositional reasoning that current architectures still find hard
    • Connecting generative models to planning and action for embodied agents
  • Discrete diffusion and reasoning
    • Inference: which signal tells a model that a token is safe to commit
    • Learning: which few tokens a model should be trained on
    • Evaluation: whether a correct answer survives an equivalent rewrite of the problem

My papers are listed on Google Scholar. You can reach me at sunwoo [at] kaist [dot] ac [dot] kr.

Pivot-SD: Efficient Self-Distillation for Masked Diffusion Language Models

Seo Hyun Kim*, Sunwoo Hong*, Younwoo Choi, Chen-Hao Chao, Se-Young Yun, Rahul G. Krishnan

EMNLP 2026 · Main Conference

A masked diffusion language model can be post-trained on under 4% of its tokens. Supervising only the few commitments that most reduce uncertainty beats full-sequence fine-tuning and online RL at a fraction of the compute.

KLASS: KL-Guided Fast Inference in Masked Diffusion Models

Seo Hyun Kim*, Sunwoo Hong*, Hojung Jung, Youngrok Park, Se-Young Yun

NeurIPS 2025

A training-free sampler that uses the KL divergence between denoising steps to find tokens that are safe to unmask in parallel. Up to 2.78× faster generation while improving over greedy decoding, across text, image, and molecular generation.

Logical Equivariance in Diffusion Language Models

Sunwoo Hong*, Seo Hyun Kim*, Chen-Hao Chao, Younwoo Choi, Se-Young Yun, Rahul G. Krishnan

Under review at ICLR 2027 · ICML 2026 Workshop on Foundations of Deep Generative Models

Accuracy on one presentation of a problem hides failures on logically equivalent ones. We measure whether correctness survives answer-preserving rewrites, and a post-training recipe raises the all-view correctness of LLaDA-8B from 47.7% to 86.1%.

Chan Park, Sunwoo Hong, Kirak Kim, Yoonjeong Park, Alexander W. Olson, Ali Pesaranghader
